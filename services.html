RECOMMENDED SERVICE INTERVALS

Keep your Bajaj motorcycle performing like new by following the authorized service milestones.

MILESTONE 1

1st Free Service

500 – 750 km or 30–45 Days

Engine oil replacement, chain lubrication, tappet & clutch adjustment, multi-point safety check.

MILESTONE 2

2nd Free Service

4,500 – 5,000 km or 240 Days

Air filter cleaning, spark plug inspection, brake pad wear check, electrical diagnostics.
